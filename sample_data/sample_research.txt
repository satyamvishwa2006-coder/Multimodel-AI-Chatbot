# Next-Generation Solid-State Battery Research (2026 Report)

## Executive Summary
Solid-state lithium batteries (SSBs) represent the most significant leap in electrical energy storage since the commercialization of liquid electrolyte lithium-ion cells in 1991. By replacing volatile organic liquid electrolytes with solid ceramic, polymer, or sulfide-based conductors, SSBs achieve volumetric energy densities exceeding 1,000 Wh/L while eliminating thermal runaway hazards up to 180°C.

## Key Performance Metrics
- **Gravimetric Energy Density**: 450 to 520 Wh/kg (compared to 260 Wh/kg in conventional NMC 811 cells).
- **Fast Charging Velocity**: 10% to 80% State of Charge (SoC) reached in 8.5 minutes under 4C pulsed charging protocols.
- **Cycle Degradation**: 91.4% capacity retention after 2,400 full equivalent cycles at 25°C.
- **Operating Temperature Range**: Stable discharge performance between -35°C and +65°C without external active thermal preheating.

## Manufacturing and Cost Outlook
Pilot roll-to-roll manufacturing facilities established in 2025 demonstrated cell-level production costs declining to $74 per kWh at scale. Dry electrode coating eliminates solvent drying ovens, reducing capital equipment expenditures by 38% and lowering factory floor footprint by 45%.
